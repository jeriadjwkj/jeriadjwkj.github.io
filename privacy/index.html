Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This policy explains how Midnight Tales ("the App") handles information. The App is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload their own videos.

Information we access

  • TikTok: your basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) and permission to upload videos to your account, granted through TikTok Login Kit.
  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to your channel, granted through Google OAuth.
  • Access tokens: the login tokens issued by TikTok and Google.

How we use it

This information is used only to upload videos you have chosen to publish to your own accounts. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Storage

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. The App has no server and no database, and no data is sent anywhere other than TikTok's and Google's official APIs.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until you revoke access or delete them. You can revoke access at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions) or in your Google Account (Security → Third-party apps). Revoking access makes the stored tokens useless.

Children

The App is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be updated.
